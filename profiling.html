Copy the results to another location (optional)

When the sweep finishes, the server copies its whole folder (merged CSV, manifest, every job's output) here with a TAPIS file transfer. No account is needed: transfers are not jobs and use your TAPIS login on both systems. You only need permission to write to the folder. The original stays where it is.
